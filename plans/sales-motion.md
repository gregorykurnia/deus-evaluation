## The answer

**F&B outreach gets meetings that don't become deals, and Enhance can't get a meeting. The two need different fixes, and in both cases more cold messages won't solve it.**

- **F&B:** 100+ contacts, almost 10 meetings, 0 deals (Fact sheet, Funnel). Getting meetings isn't the problem; something after the meeting is. More contacts just add meetings that stall the same way. F&B frontline hiring scored lowest of the Discover segments (39 of 80; Step 6b), and the one F&B buyer, Boga, did not come back (Fact sheet).
- **Enhance:** hundreds of Apollo contacts, 0 meetings (Fact sheet, Funnel). Better copy won't fix that. A stranger is being asked to trust a five-person company with performance management at Rp30 juta a month, 2.5–4 times what packaged HR systems with a KPI module list for a company of DKB's size (Step 7, MC6).
- **The pattern:** every channel DEUS ran itself has closed 0 deals (Steps 1, 2, 5). Building a network of your own is the right instinct (interpretation), but that network is warm, specific people, not cold lists.

## 1. Close out the F&B meetings you have, then stop adding new contacts

Wanting this to work is not a reason to keep a channel going at 0 of 10 (CLAUDE.md, rule 3). The verdict already stops new F&B contacts (Step 8).

- **Get a clear answer from each of the ~10.** A sample message: *"Thanks again for the time on [date]. I'd rather ask than keep following up: is this something you'd want to change in the next 3 months, or not now? Either answer helps. If it's not now, could I ask you 3 questions about how you assess today? 15 minutes, no pitch."*
- **Treat the "not now" answers as research calls.** The verdict gives these to Thania (at least 7 of about 10; Step 8). No buyer has yet given reasons in their own words, and these people already know DEUS.
- **End every call with one referral ask:** "Who do you know who runs a big management-trainee (MT) or graduate intake?"
- **F&B can come back** after 2027-01-26 with a different offer, but only if the calls show a real hiring need that stopped on price, proof or game length (Step 8).

## 2. Handling "we already have an assessment" and "we don't want another layer"

Stop selling "an assessment." The buyer already has one, so the pitch reads as extra cost and extra steps. Sell taking a step away instead (interpretation).

- **Ask, don't argue:** What do you use, and at which stage? How many candidates a year go through it? What does it cost per head, and how long until results? What do you wish it told you, such as who quits in the first 3 months?
- **Rule them out fast.** A buyer content with its test is a real no (Step 3, anti-profile). Let it go in the first 10 minutes instead of spending months on "we'll discuss internally." Those long cycles are the biggest hidden cost (interpretation).
- **If there is pain, pitch less work:** "This only makes sense if it replaces something: the first psychology test, or the first screening interview for 1,000 candidates. If it doesn't replace anything, don't buy it." The proof is the Telkom intake: 1,000+ candidates, 30+ provinces at once, results in one day (Fact sheet).
- **Offer to run it alongside their current test** on one real intake at the Rp10 juta minimum order, with a pass line and a decision date agreed before the start. Only such orders count toward a pass (Step 8). With consent, the same data can feed a validation study led by a university institute.
- **Two problems that are DEUS's own:**
  - Prospects call the game long, with small text (Fact sheet, Funnel). That makes "another layer" a fair complaint; fix it before pushing harder.
  - The personality game has no validation, and **nobody in the team is a psychologist** (Fact sheet, Validation; Correction 6). With buyers who employ psychologists, lead with the cognitive module, which has a validation, and say plainly what is not validated.
- **Sell through the people who said "we have our own":** psychology bureaus, Telkom's Assessment Center as a reseller, and university institutes (the channels plan, #1–3). They bring the licensed psychologist DEUS lacks. Indonesia's psychology code of ethics may also require one for psychological assessment (unverified; the channels plan, W7).

## 4. Building a network of your own

The 5 named introducers due at Gate 1 (2026-11-11; Step 8) don't have to come from the dad. Fastest first:

1. **People who've seen DEUS work.** List every HR person who used DEUS at Telkom's Assessment Center, Boga, DKB, SPH and Calvin, and find where they are now on LinkedIn. Someone who used DEUS at Telkom and has moved companies is the warmest lead DEUS has (interpretation).
2. **Psychologists outside DEUS**, since none is inside (Correction 6): the psychologists at Telkom's Assessment Center, a direct pitch to university institutes such as LPTUI (the channels plan, #2), and whoever ran the validation of Agate's Next Chapter (Agate built it with a research institute; Step 4, W8), through an introduction from Agate.
3. **Referrals** from the F&B research calls (section 1).
4. **A small roundtable DEUS hosts** for 8–12 people who run MT or graduate intakes, with Telkom's Assessment Center as co-speaker on the Telkom MT case. People accept an invitation more often than a pitch, and everyone who attends is warm afterwards (ASSUMPTION; cost NO DATA).
5. **Slow but builds up:** one LinkedIn post a week from the founder, in Indonesian, on what running 1,000-candidate intakes taught; PASSTI and assessment-centre events (the channels plan, #4).

**Audrey's time** moves, if F&B stops, to the list of companies with open MT or graduate intakes and their dates, tracking down past-client contacts, and organising the roundtable. **If any cold outreach stays**, it goes only to companies with an MT intake open now, from the founder personally, about that intake, with a pass line: 30 messages, 3 meetings with a dated intake, or it stops.

## Key findings

1. **F&B's problem is after the meeting, not before it:** about 10 meetings and 0 deals, so more contacts add more stalled meetings. Close out the ten, learn from them, stop adding.
2. **"We already have an assessment" is answered by subtraction, not persuasion:** pitch the stage DEUS removes, rule out content buyers in 10 minutes, and sell through those who already have assessments.
3. **Enhance can't be sold cold** at 2.5–4 times packaged HR-system prices from a five-person company; protect DKB and try only warm referrals.
4. **DEUS has no psychologist** (Correction 6), so validity claims, validation and psychologist sign-off must come from Agate, institutes or bureaus. That also makes them the natural channel.
5. **Your own network is warm and specific:** past-client contacts who moved jobs, outside psychologists, referrals and a roundtable you host, not cold lists.
