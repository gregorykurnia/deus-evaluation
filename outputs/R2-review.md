## Is the 30-day plan falsifiable?

Yes after the fixes. Each test has a dated pass line that money, not opinion, settles; "anything short of a pass
is a fail"; the default branch (shrink) is written; and the clock has its own rule. Before the fixes, three holes
could have let the plan drift, which is the loop Steps 1 and 2 diagnosed:

- A day-30 result of exactly 1 meeting (test A) or 1 proposal (test B) was neither on track nor off track. Under
  the moved clock only an on-track test may continue, so it matters.
- A test could pass its money line without the right to sell (Agate terms, DKB's contract), and the decision
  table then had no row for it.
- Test B's 30-day target (10 introductions by 2026-11-27) did not fit its own quota (2 a week from week 2, about 6).
