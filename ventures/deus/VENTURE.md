# Venture: DEUS

- **One-liner:** Indonesian HR-tech company selling game-based assessment (Discover) and performance management software (Enhance) to employers, and a gamified cognitive assessment for kids (Potentia) to schools and parents. Why better: not shown yet. Candidate (outputs/04 §2.2, §4): DEUS's own game, report and large-intake delivery (Telkom: 1,000+ candidates across 30+ provinces, results in one day). The Next Chapter modules are also sold by four other Agate partners.
- **Type:** own
- **Stage:** launched
- **Target market & geography:** Indonesia. Discover: central assessment units of large groups that run a yearly, high-volume intake and have an open search for a gamified assessment (the one evidenced buyer, Telkom's Assessment Center, reached through Agate; outputs/03 §2). Outreach so far (F&B and finance cold lists, Apollo for Enhance) found no deal. Enhance: groups with complex KPI and appraisal systems; one client (DKB), no ICP; run as a service contract, not sold to the market (outputs/03 §3, outputs/04 §4). Potentia: schools and parents; recommended for exit (outputs/04 §4). Focus: not decided by the founder. Revised Step 4 proposes Discover narrowly, Enhance for DKB, Potentia out; the review's tests were set on first-pass facts and are stale.
- **Business type:** other: B2B assessment and HR software, plus Potentia (per child, sold on the website and to schools)
- **Report language:** en
- **Created:** 2026-10-28
- **Last updated:** 2026-10-28 (founder answers)
