## The answer in one paragraph

**Neither product has product-market fit yet.** Discover's best evidence is one account, Telkom, that bought two yearly MT intakes and then stopped or paused for reasons nobody has recorded; no Discover buyer pays today, and every segment tried since converted zero (Recap, Phases 7–11; Fact sheet). Enhance has one paying relationship (DKB, 100% of revenue), one churned client and zero meetings from hundreds of cold contacts (Fact sheet; Recap, Phases 8, 11). The three stalled funnels share one cause the files support well: DEUS sold to people who had no hiring event or problem in hand, and never recorded why they said no. For Discover the likely gap is **the wrong segment** (a hypothesis: the Telkom-like segment has not been approached since Phase 7), with an equally serious open question: **whether DEUS is replaceable**, since Agate sells the same games directly and incumbents already serve Telkom-group firms (Agate deck; Step 4). The 30-day plan starts with the cheapest evidence (one Telkom call, ten F&B loss calls, DEUS's Agate terms), then tests 20–30 Telkom-like accounts on leading indicators and asks DKB's own users whether they would miss Enhance. Decision date: 2026-11-27.

## 3. Why the funnel stalled: three questions

The founder asked for three explanations. None of the three has a recorded reason from a buyer (Fact sheet, Founder notes: "What reasons did lost leads give for not buying? ?"). What follows are the facts, then the most likely explanations ranked by how well the files support them. Every cause is **interpretation** until the calls in section 8 run.

### 3.1 Why F&B responses did not convert

**Facts.** 100+ F&B contacts, "a good number of responses", almost 10 meetings, 0 deals (Recap, Phase 11; Fact sheet). The pitch used was the new game suite (Recap, Phase 11) and the decks sell culture fit for any hire, with packages from 5 to 100 candidates and a free trial (Pitch deck, slides 1–3, A8–A9; Step 3 section 5). DEUS's one earlier F&B buyer, Boga, bought an internal assessment of 600 staff at about Rp100,000 each, did not repeat and "did not seem fully satisfied" (Recap, Phase 2; Fact sheet).

| Likely cause (interpretation) | Support in the files | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| **No hiring event behind the meeting.** The prospects took a meeting because games are interesting, not because a deadline needed a tool. Responses without a trigger are curiosity, not demand | Every buyer who paid arrived with the need in hand; every channel where DEUS had to create the need converted almost nothing (Step 3, key finding 2; L01–L07) | Strongest: fits all six channels |
| **Per-candidate price against frontline hiring budgets.** F&B volume hiring is mostly frontline staff, where Rp69,000–279,000 per candidate (Pitch deck, A9) is hard to justify | ASSUMPTION about F&B hiring (Step 3 section 5); Boga's "low price" and one-off purchase is the one data point | Plausible, untested |
| **The pitch sells a different job.** "Fit the role, they perform; fit your company, they stay" asks the buyer to imagine a benefit; the only repeat buyer bought a one-day screen of 1,000+ MT candidates | Pitch deck slides 1–3 vs W04; the GBA deck lists "Annual MT Recruitment" first, but the F&B outreach did not use it (Step 3 section 5) | Plausible |
| **Nobody followed up for a reason.** With no loss reason asked or recorded, the team cannot tell which of the above it was | Fact sheet, Founder notes | Certain as a gap, not as a cause |

**The product is not ruled out.** No F&B prospect is recorded as having tried the new suite and rejected it, but the full suite is 3 h 20 min of play (Pitch deck slide 7; Step 3), long for frontline hiring (ASSUMPTION). "A good number of responses" is the strongest sign in Phase 11 that the message opens doors; it is also the most likely vanity metric in this report.

### 3.2 Why finance replies are low

**Facts.** About 100 finance contacts, "not many" replies; meetings and deals NO DATA (Recap, Phase 11; Fact sheet). The contacts' roles and how the list was built: NO DATA.

| Likely cause (interpretation) | Support in the files | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| **Incumbents already serve large finance firms.** Talentlytica and Talentics name BRI, Bank Indonesia, Bank Mandiri and BNI among their clients | Step 4 section 2.2 (W3, the competitors' own claims) | Plausible for large banks |
| **Finance buyers ask for validity evidence first.** The original game had no psychometric validation (Recap, Phase 1); which games the decks' "validated" and "Indonesian norms" claims cover is unclear (Step 2, data gaps) | ASSUMPTION that regulated firms ask for it before a meeting (Step 3, deal-breakers) | Plausible, untested |

**Interpretation.** Finance is the first segment DEUS chose that could contain Telkom-like buyers. The low reply rate says the cold message did not reach or did not move them; it does not yet say finance is wrong. Section 8 (M4) splits the list to find out.

**Facts.** "Hundreds" of contacts, 0 meetings (Recap, Phase 11; Fact sheet). The two Enhance buyers both signed while the product was unfinished, one through the dad's relationship (Recap, Phase 3). Enhance has no published price and no case study (Fact sheet, Pricing; Step 3).

## 4. Fit segment analysis

- **Discover:** the only account that ever repeated (and has since lapsed or paused) is a large national group with an **annual, high-volume MT intake** (1,000+ candidates, Telkom plus 6 subsidiaries, 30+ provinces, results in 1 day) that was **already looking for a gamified vendor** (W04; Pitch deck; Recap, Phase 7). The account that did not retain bought an internal assessment of existing staff at a low price (W01). The segments tried since (F&B, finance, the dad's retail network) are neither: none is defined by a hiring event, and none arrived with the need (Step 3, sections 2 and 5). Fit, if it exists, lives in the first subset, and what that account praised was a **service-led MT assessment project** run by a team most of whom have since left (Bu Retno, Wulan and Sahid left; Audrey remains; who delivered Telkom is NO DATA; Recap, Phases 7–8, Reference: people), using the single personality game, not today's 3 h 20 min suite (Step 3, deal-breakers; Pitch deck slide 7). It rests on one account. Telkom group now also takes part in the joint state-company recruitment (Rekrutmen Bersama BUMN 2025; [investortrust, 13 March 2025](https://investortrust.id/business/59560/rekrutmen-bersama-bumn-2025-dibuka-telkomgroup-ajak-talenta-terbaik-bergabung)); whether its MT intake still buys outside assessment is NO DATA.
- **Enhance:** the account that retained (DKB) is held by a family relationship and by features built on request (Step 3). The account that left (BCG) left over quality and data handling (C01). Neither describes a market segment; both describe custom-system clients.

**Interpretation.** Read together, the cases say the same thing as Step 3: the buyers who paid had a dated, structured HR process and a problem already in hand (an MT intake; a complex KPI and appraisal system). Neither product has shown pull from anyone without that.

## 6. Verdict and diagnosis

### Verdict

**NO FIT YET, for both products as sold today.**

- **Discover:** one lapsed proof point, not a fit segment. 1 of 3 buyers ever repeated, 0 of 3 pay today; every segment approached since Telkom converted 0 (scorecard signals 1, 6; Fact sheet; Recap, Phases 7–11). What Telkom praised was the service (GBA deck, Success Story).
- **Enhance:** one relationship, not fit. 1 of 2 buyers retained, 0 meetings from hundreds of cold contacts, no inbound, no usage data (signals 1, 4, 6; Fact sheet; Recap, Phases 8, 11).
- Confidence in the verdict: **medium**. Confidence in any *positive* reading: **low**: the signals that could show fit (Sean Ellis, usage, loss reasons) have never been measured.

### Diagnosis (the skill's gap ladder)

| | Discover | Enhance |
|---|---|---|
| Rung | **① wrong segment, hypothesised**; ② messaging close behind | **① no defined segment**; ③ for BCG (quality) |
| Co-equal question outside the ladder | **Is DEUS replaceable?** Agate sells the same suite directly at Rp49,999–300,000 per user with free pilots (Agate deck); incumbents name Telkom-group and state-bank clients (Step 4, sections 2.2, 5) | Same question: incumbents sell performance modules inside full HR systems with thousands of claimed clients (Step 4, section 2.2) |

| What would move it | Telkom-like accounts saying no for product reasons → ③; no meetings even with the right buyer and message → ④; buyers naming Agate or an incumbent on price → "replaceable" | DKB's users not valuing it (M5) → DKB is a relationship, not a product client |
| Confirming experiment | Telkom call + F&B loss calls (weeks 1–2), then Step 2 Experiment 1 with shortlist tracking (section 8) | DKB user survey (M5) now; Step 2 Experiment 2 after 2026-11-27 |

## Key findings

1. **No fit yet for either product; nothing passes on the scorecard** (Discover: 2 fail, 2 borderline, 2 not measured; Enhance: 3 fail, 1 borderline, 3 not measured). The two signals the method weights most, retention and the Sean Ellis survey, rest on 2–3 buyers or have never been measured (section 2).
2. **Discover's only proof point has lapsed.** Telkom bought two MT intakes in Phase 7 and nothing is recorded since; 0 Discover buyers pay today (Fact sheet). What its selection lead praised was DEUS's professionalism, the service (GBA deck, Success Story), and Telkom group now also uses the joint state-company recruitment (investortrust, 2025). Why it stopped is the cheapest, most important unknown in the company.
3. **The three stalled funnels share one cause: no hiring event or problem in hand, and no loss reason recorded.** F&B's "good number" of responses reads as curiosity; finance was an industry list, not a buyer list; Apollo pitched a product with no defined buyer into a market where incumbents claim thousands of clients (section 3; Step 3; Step 4).
4. **Diagnosis: wrong segment (hypothesised) for Discover, co-equal with "DEUS may be replaceable"**: Agate sells the same suite directly with free pilots, and incumbents already serve Telkom-group firms (Agate deck; Step 4). Enhance has no defined segment; BCG left over quality (section 6).
5. **The 30-day plan asks before it sells:** a Telkom call and DEUS's Agate terms (the gate), ten F&B loss calls, then 20–30 Telkom-like accounts scored on leading indicators with every shortlist recorded, and a Sean Ellis survey of DKB's users. Decision date 2026-11-27 (section 8).
