## Fit segment analysis

- **Discover:** the only account that ever repeated (and has since lapsed or paused) is a large national group with an **annual, high-volume MT intake** (1,000+ candidates, Telkom plus 6 subsidiaries, 30+ provinces, results in 1 day) that was **already looking for a gamified vendor** (W04; Pitch deck; Recap, Phase 7). The account that did not retain bought an internal assessment of existing staff at a low price (W01). The segments tried since (F&B, finance, the dad's retail network) are neither: none is defined by a hiring event, and none arrived with the need (Step 3, sections 2 and 5). Fit, if it exists, lives in the first subset, and what that account praised was a **service-led MT assessment project** run by a team most of whom have since left (Bu Retno, Wulan and Sahid left; Audrey remains; who delivered Telkom is NO DATA; Recap, Phases 7–8, Reference: people), using the single personality game, not today's 3 h 20 min suite (Step 3, deal-breakers; Pitch deck slide 7). It rests on one account. Telkom group now also takes part in the joint state-company recruitment (Rekrutmen Bersama BUMN 2025; [investortrust, 13 March 2025](https://investortrust.id/business/59560/rekrutmen-bersama-bumn-2025-dibuka-telkomgroup-ajak-talenta-terbaik-bergabung)); whether its MT intake still buys outside assessment is NO DATA.
- **Enhance:** the account that retained (DKB) is held by a family relationship and by features built on request (Step 3). The account that left (BCG) left over quality and data handling (C01). Neither describes a market segment; both describe custom-system clients.

## Verdict

**NO FIT YET for either product as sold today.** Discover has one lapsed proof point (Telkom), not a fit segment; Enhance has one relationship (DKB), which is not evidence of fit.
- Discover: 1 of 3 buyers ever repeated, 0 of 3 pay today; every segment tried since Telkom converted 0 (signals 1, 6).
- Enhance: 1 of 2 buyers retained, 0 meetings from hundreds of cold contacts, no inbound (signals 1, 4, 6).
- The two signals the skill weights most (retention curves and Sean Ellis) are either n ≤ 3 or not measured at all.

## Diagnosis & next move

**Discover.** Gap ladder position: **① wrong segment, hypothesised** (F&B, finance and the network were chosen by reach, not by the Telkom evidence; but the Telkom-like segment has not been approached since Phase 7, so ① has no more direct support than ②–④). **Co-equal, outside the ladder: DEUS may be replaceable** (Agate sells the same game suite directly at Rp49,999–300,000 per user with free pilots; incumbents already name Telkom-group and state-bank clients; Agate deck; Step 4 sections 2.2, 5). ② messaging is close behind ①. ③ and ④ cannot be ruled out.
Confirming experiment: first the cheapest evidence, a call to Telkom (why no third year) and loss-reason calls to the ~10 F&B prospects (Step 2 Experiment 3), in weeks 1–2; then Step 2 Experiment 1 (20–30 named Telkom-like accounts, recording each prospect's shortlist and the price gap to Agate), with a 30-day checkpoint on leading indicators in `pmf-measurement-plan-2026-10-28.md`.

**Enhance.** Gap ladder position: **① no defined segment** plus the same competitive question (incumbents with thousands of claimed clients show the pain exists in the market, so ④ does not fit; what is missing is a buyer who would choose Enhance; Step 3, key finding 4; Step 4 section 2.2), and **③ for the one buyer who left** (BCG: quality). Confirming test: a Sean Ellis survey of DKB's own users (n ≥ 30) to measure whether the one account values the product or the relationship, and Step 2 Experiment 2 (10 DKB-like companies, fixed price) after the Discover checkpoint.

## Recommendation

- **Discover: narrow to segment X, after a gate** (large groups with an annual MT, graduate or campus intake of several hundred candidates or more, sold as a service-led project). Gate: Telkom's reason for no third year and DEUS's Agate terms, both in week 1. No new builds.
- **Enhance: structured hold** as a priced DKB service contract (Step 4); no new outreach until the DKB survey and Experiment 2 say otherwise.
- **Potentia:** not scored; Step 4's exit stands.
