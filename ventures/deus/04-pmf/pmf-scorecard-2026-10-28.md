## Fit segment analysis

- **Discover:** the only account that ever repeated is **a central assessment unit of a large group with a high-volume MT intake (1,000+ candidates, Telkom plus 6 subsidiaries, 30+ provinces, results in 1 day) that went looking for a gamified assessment and asked Agate** (W04; Pitch deck; Corrections 2–3). It lasted as long as the programme: when Telkom changed its MT intake strategy, the buying stopped. The account that did not retain bought an internal assessment of existing staff at a low price and now has its own systems (W01). The segments tried since (F&B, finance, the dad's retail network, cold Telkom-like lists) had no open search, and the reason prospects give most, "we already have an assessment", fits that reading, though it also fits ③ (section 3.1; Founder answers). What Telkom praised was a **service-led MT assessment project** run by a team most of whom have since left (Bu Retno, Wulan and Sahid left; Audrey remains; who delivered it: NO DATA; Recap, Phases 7–8, Reference: people), using the single personality game, not today's 3 h 20 min suite (Pitch deck, slide 7). Fit, if it exists, lives in **buyers with an open search, reached through the vendor they ask**. In Phase 7 that vendor was Agate, before Agate launched Next Chapter as its own product (Recap, Phase 11; W8, June 2026). Today Agate sells Next Chapter through five partners on the founder's account (Correction 1), its launch page has its own business-development team scoping pilots (W8), and it has run a Telkomsel assessment itself (W4). Whether Agate would pass DEUS an open-search lead now is unverified.
- **Enhance:** the account that retained (DKB: 300–400 employees with access, "quite satisfied" on the founder's account) came through the dad and gets features built on request (Step 3; Fact sheet); whether the relationship or the product holds it is unknown. The account that left (BCG) had the need and left over service, on the founder's account (Correction 4). Neither describes a market segment; both describe custom-system clients of a family network.

## Devil's advocate findings

Six objections, verdict "survives with downgrades"; five accepted in full, one in part (Discover signal 7 kept borderline at low confidence). Main changes: the Agate route marked unverified with a possible conflict of interest (Agate's own business-development team scopes Next Chapter pilots, W8, checked 2026-10-28); for assessment units, validation and "why DEUS" put level with the wrong-segment rung; price restored as an open cause; Discover signals 3 and 4 downgraded; DKB relabelled a retained account (relationship or product unknown) and measured by server logs first; the plan gained a kill row, a stricter Agate gate and independent loss calls. Detail: `pmf-assessment-2026-10-28.md` section 5.

## Verdict

- **Discover: NO FIT YET.** One proof point (Telkom's Assessment Center, reached through Agate, stopped when its MT intake strategy changed, on the founder's account), not a fit segment. 0 of 3 buyers pay today; every channel DEUS ran itself converted 0 (signals 1, 4, 6).
- **Enhance as a market product: NO FIT.** 0 meetings from hundreds of cold contacts; no takers at Rp30 juta among warm contacts; BCG left over service (signals 1, 4, 6).
- **Enhance for DKB: a retained account, not a measured fit.** Relationship or product: unknown.

## Diagnosis & next move

**Discover.** In the channels tried: **① wrong segment** (buyers without an open search; the founder's most common loss reason is "we already have an assessment"). In the segment Step 4 backs (assessment units): **③ validation and experience level with ①** (the personality game DEUS owns has no validation; the game is "a bit long" with small text), and **"why DEUS?"** co-equal (four other Agate partners; Agate's own team; incumbents). "Already have one" fits ① and ③ alike.
Confirming experiment: the Agate gate (written lead rule by 2026-11-11), Step 2 Experiments 1 and 3, coded loss reasons in buyers' words, and a kill row (programme owners met who say "already have one" or ask for validation) in `pmf-measurement-plan-2026-10-28.md`.

**Enhance.** Market: **① no defined segment** plus a price about 2.5–4 times packaged HRIS lists (Step 4, W10). BCG: **③ under-delivery** (service). DKB: measure use from DEUS's own server logs and ask the decision-maker what they would do without it; log DKB hours for 30 days (Step 2, Experiment 2).

## Recommendation

- **Discover: narrow to buyers with an open search, through the channel they use,** behind the Agate gate. No new builds; shorter module sets and readable text are not builds.
- **Enhance: structured stop as a market product;** keep DKB as a service contract with a written scope (Step 2 section 6; Step 4).
- **Potentia:** not scored; Step 4's exit stands.
